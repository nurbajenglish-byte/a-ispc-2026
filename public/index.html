Frequently Asked Questions

+ What does "AI-assisted" mean? Can we use any AI tool?
AI should be a genuine contributor to your research — not a gimmick. Use it for data analysis, literature reviews, hypothesis generation, visualization, or hypothesis refinement. The key is that you document how you used it, justify your choice, and critically evaluate its role. LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude), computer vision systems, data platforms, and specialized research tools all count. What doesn't count: having AI write your report for you or using it only for formatting.
+ Can students from different schools form a team?
No. All team members must be enrolled at the same school, and your mentor teacher must also be a staff member of that school. This ensures clear institutional accountability and simplifies coordination. Schools can enter multiple teams — just register each one separately.
+ What is the refund policy?
Full refunds are available until August 31, 2026. After that date, registration fees are non-refundable as judging resources are committed based on team counts. If A-ISPC 2026 is cancelled by the organizers, all fees will be refunded in full.
+ Do we have to travel to Hong Kong for the finals?
No. The finals are hybrid — qualifying teams can present in person in Hong Kong or via live video link. Physical attendance is encouraged but not required. Remote participants have full judging access and awards eligibility.
+ In what currency is the entry fee? What about currency conversion?
Entry fees are USD $70 (early bird) or $80 (standard). Payment is collected via Stripe by Emerald Horizon Ventures (Hong Kong). Your card will be charged in your local currency at the rate applied by Stripe or your card issuer. A receipt in USD will be issued to your mentor teacher's email.
+ What are the judging criteria?
Teams are evaluated on three dimensions: (1) Scientific Rigour — does your methodology stand up? (2) Creativity of Inquiry — is your research question original? (3) Thoughtful AI Integration — did you use AI meaningfully and document its role? The balance is 40% rigour, 30% creativity, 30% AI integration.
+ We're having trouble registering. Who do we contact?
Email our registration team at register@aispc2026.org. We aim to respond within 2 business days. For technical issues with the registration form, include a screenshot if possible.
+ Can our school get bulk discounts for multiple teams?
Not on the early bird rate, as that's a time-limited offer. However, for schools registering 5+ teams, we offer institutional pricing. Contact us at register@aispc2026.org for a custom quote.
