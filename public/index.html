About A-ISPC

Science × Artificial Intelligence

A-ISPC challenges secondary school students to conduct original scientific investigations that incorporate AI tools — from data analysis and pattern recognition to literature synthesis and hypothesis generation.

This isn't about building AI. It's about doing better science with AI. Teams are judged on the rigour of their scientific method, the creativity of their inquiry, and the thoughtfulness of their AI integration.

The competition begins with an online elimination round, with qualifying teams invited to present at the hybrid finals in Hong Kong in December 2026.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the Early Bird discount?

Teams that register and pay before 31 July 2026 receive the Early Bird rate of USD $70 per team. From 1 August 2026, the standard rate of USD $80 applies. The discount is applied automatically at checkout — no code needed.

What does "AI-Assisted" mean in practice?

Teams should use AI tools — such as large language models, computer vision systems, or data analysis platforms — as genuine contributors to their research process. This might include using AI to analyse experimental data, conduct literature reviews, generate visualisations, or assist with hypothesis refinement. The key is that the AI's role must be documented, justified, and critically evaluated in your report.

Can students from different schools form a team?

No. All team members must be enrolled at the same school, and the team's mentor teacher must also be a staff member of that school. This rule ensures clear institutional accountability.

What is the refund policy?

Full refunds are available until 31 August 2026. After that date, the registration fee is non-refundable as judging resources are committed based on registered team counts. In the event that A-ISPC 2026 is cancelled by the organisers, all fees will be refunded in full.

Does every team member need to travel to Hong Kong?

The finals are hybrid. Qualifying teams may attend in person in Hong Kong or present remotely via live video link. Physical attendance is strongly encouraged but we recognise that travel may not be possible for all teams — remote participants have full judging access and awards eligibility.

In what currency is payment collected?

The entry fee is USD $70 (Early Bird) or USD $80 (Standard). Payment is collected via Stripe by Emerald Horizon Ventures (Hong Kong). Your card will be charged in your local currency at the rate applied by Stripe or your card issuer. A receipt in USD will be issued to your mentor teacher's email.

We're having trouble registering. Who do we contact?

Email our registration team at register@aispc2026.org. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
